The author's recomputations and the lineage's evidence files, in the repository's `data/` directory:

- [`data/lineage-commits.md`](https://github.com/gfrmin/agent-court/blob/master/data/lineage-commits.md) — the predecessors' commit histories, turning points as their own subjects name them.
- [`data/lineage-sessions.md`](https://github.com/gfrmin/agent-court/blob/master/data/lineage-sessions.md) — the owner's dated words on each predecessor, from the sessions and notes on his machine.
- [`data/counsel-notes.md`](https://github.com/gfrmin/agent-court/blob/master/data/counsel-notes.md) — counsel's dispositions and evidence calendar (counsel's file).
- `data/<layer>-exit.md` — counsel's exit evidence for each window, as it lands.
